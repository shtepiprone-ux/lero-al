# Sprint 45 — the unsprinted period (Tasks 621–705)

**Span:** 2026-07-17 → 2026-08-01. **State:** 🟢 **CLOSED.** **Epic:** mostly MM (Mantine/TailAdmin) Phase-2, with
product and governance slices mixed in.

> **This is not a sprint that was planned — it is a period that was named after the fact.**
>
> Sprints 0–44 were planned up front and ended at **Task 620**. Tasks **621–705** then ran as standalone tasks with
> no sprint above them for roughly six weeks, and nobody noticed until the 2026-08-01 backlog audit. One visible
> consequence: `docs/mantine-tailadmin-migration-tracker.md` went on pointing at slices that had been finished for a
> month, because no sprint boundary ever forced it to be re-read.
>
> A first attempt at reconstruction split this period into eight themed "sprints" 45–52. That was rejected and
> deleted: the resulting ranges **overlapped in nine places** — Sprint 51 sat entirely inside Sprint 50 — because the
> only dates available are *review* dates, not work dates, and several tasks were reviewed in batches long after they
> were implemented. A sprint is a time box; inventing eight of them that run in parallel would have been fiction
> presented as history.
>
> So this file records the period as what it actually was: **one unsprinted stretch, closed as one unit.** The themes
> below are a reading aid for navigating it, not sprint boundaries. Kickoff files stay at
> `tasks/kickoff_prompt_Task_*.md` — they were **not** moved, so every path reference in the ledger and the session
> logs remains valid.
>
> **Sprint discipline restarts clean at Sprint 46.** From **Task 706** onward a task may not be created without a
> sprint (`CLAUDE.md`, `.claude/skills/create-task/SKILL.md`, `docs/ai-behavior.md`).
