## Where the link leads (navigate to the href, read location only; no account chosen, nothing submitted)
host accounts.google.com, path /v3/signin/accountchooser ("Choose an account to continue to Medium")
params: response_type=id_token token; scope=email openid profile; redirect_uri=https://medium.com/m/callback/google;
        access_type=online; flowName=GeneralOAuthFlow; a nonce parameter is present (value redacted by the tool); client_id 216296035834… (truncated)
Reading: a full-page redirect to Google's OpenID Connect authorization endpoint with response_type id_token (implicit flow with a nonce),
returning to Medium's own callback on its own domain. No client secret is needed for an id_token response.
curl of the same link without a browser: HTTP 403 (rv11-01).
