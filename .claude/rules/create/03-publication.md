# Opus task design: publication gate

## C6. Challenge and validate the complete route

Read and complete `shared/task-readiness.md` for the final saved task, including its affected-path manifest, actual
executor authority, command timing, proof sensitivity and every affected sidecar. Open the saved dependencies and
attempt the counterexample most likely to invalidate each changed premise; do not merely reread your own draft.
Retain actual observations and failing/passing controls in the design evidence record. A successful `plan` or evidence
checker does not substitute for these checks. Before publishing, also confirm:

- `shared/canonical-component-reuse.md` was applied first; inherited ready design and unchanged chrome follow its
  explicit research/parity exception rather than a fresh audit or measurement receipt;

- the adjacent `.security.json` contract from `shared/workflow-gates.md` matches the final kickoff hash and passes
  the draft's in-memory ready-candidate validation, then the final saved `plan` after promotion under
  `shared/orchestration-state.md`; future tests remain `planned`;

- `npm run check:kickoff-evidence` passes for the saved kickoff; every material task premise resolves to a `C# FACT`
  or `D# OWNER DECISION`, and no inference/unknown/assumption drives the implementation route;
- the evidence and rule-compliance records match the final task text; assertions are facts, not deferred guesses;
- exactly one implementation route remains; owner choices are explicit `BLOCKED` decisions, not alternative routes;
- every required command is known, native Windows PowerShell-ready when owner-run, and has expected evidence;
- UI scope includes the source-backed Mobile adaptation record, selected registry-allowed Mantine mechanism and
  canonical owner, canonical decision, direct Story, token contract, parity receipt, GR-3j compact-range and
  640px-transition verification, and owner matrix; no task may use the contract to replace Mantine behavior;
- permanent Story work is a real canonical/prod need, while probes have restoration evidence;
- sprint/reserved/backlog state is synchronized and `check:backlog-active` passes where required;
- a fresh Sonnet can implement without chat context.

**Exit:** every applicable readiness check passes on the final content. If not, keep the route unpublishable, name
the failed check and continue investigation. Never hand off "let Sonnet find out" instructions.

## C7. Publish only the version that was verified

Reopen the saved kickoff after writing and verify headings, references, requirement/AC IDs, route, and amendment
inventory. Then inspect status/diff and provide an explicit-path owner `git add` plus `git commit` handoff for files
Opus changed. The commit is an unattributed owner commit: no `Co-Authored-By`, AI/agent credit, or `--author` may
appear in its command or message. Task design never emits `git push`. Follow the detailed lock/status procedure in
`docs/orchestrator-procedures.md`.

Record final task/contract and input digests and the readiness evidence locator. Follow the candidate recheck in
`shared/orchestration-state.md` immediately before handoff, including new dependencies. Recheck status, latest
relevant commit and existence. Rerun invalidated checks and their dependents first; a receipt cannot refresh proof.
